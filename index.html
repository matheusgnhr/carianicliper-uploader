CarianiCliper Uploader

An internal, single-operator tool (internal system name: ClipOps) that runs locally on the operator's own computer in Brazil and publishes short vertical videos (Shorts) to the operator's own YouTube channel, CarianiCliper.

Uses YouTube API Services

What it does

  • Prepares short vertical clips (9:16) from the operator's own content or from Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) videos, always with credit to the original author, a link to the source and original commentary.
  • Every clip is reviewed and approved by a human before upload. A rights check runs before approval and again right before upload.
  • Uploads to the operator's own channel through the YouTube Data API (OAuth), and reads the status of those uploads and the channel's own aggregate metrics.
  • Safety controls: kill switch, per-channel pause, daily limit of 1–3 uploads per channel.
  • Runs only on the operator's computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest in a local database; no data is sold or shared.
